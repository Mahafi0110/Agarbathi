Our story

Shubham Pooja began with a simple idea: the incense used in daily worship should smell calm, burn evenly and be easy to buy, whether you need one pack for the pooja room or a hundred for a shop.

Today we offer sandalwood, floral, masala and dhoop ranges, packed carefully and priced fairly for both retail and wholesale buyers.

What we stand for

  • Fragrances chosen for daily worship, not for show.
  • Clear pack sizes and honest descriptions on every product.
  • One catalogue for retail and wholesale, with the bulk rate shown up front.
  • A phone number that is answered when you have a question.

Retail and wholesale

Retail: buy single packs for your home, with Cash on Delivery or online payment.

Wholesale: shops, temples and event organisers get a bulk rate on each product, plus cartons, assortments and private label on request.
